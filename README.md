\# MerchantOps AI



\## Your AI Operations Team for Smarter Merchant Decisions



MerchantOps AI is an autonomous merchant decision-intelligence platform that helps online merchants detect revenue risks, analyze payment and business signals, assess operational risk, simulate possible interventions, and make intelligent decisions through coordinated AI agents.



The platform is designed to follow an observe → diagnose → predict → simulate → decide → approve → act → verify workflow.



\## Core Focus



\- Payment intelligence

\- Revenue recovery

\- Risk analysis

\- AI-driven decision support

\- Action simulation

\- Controlled automation

\- Auditability



\## Core AI Agents



\- Revenue Agent

\- Risk Agent

\- Simulation Agent

\- Decision Agent

\- Orchestrator



\## Planned Integrations



MerchantOps AI will integrate with payment infrastructure using a Razorpay test environment for development and demonstration.



\## Project Goal



Build a practical, explainable and auditable AI system that does more than provide recommendations: it evaluates possible actions, selects an appropriate intervention, supports controlled execution, and verifies the outcome.
